# DREAM PROTOCOL: updates

Every update has a name. The newest is at the top, and the title screen shows the current one.

## Update 7: "Mind the Gap"
An emergency fix: walls had gone missing in places, and you could walk off the edge of the world.
- **THE DEEP**: the lobby and the changing rooms had their street-side walls on the wrong side. You could walk straight out of the lobby into nothing. Fixed, street doors and all.
- **The Backrooms**: the doorway where the service passage comes back into the yellow rooms was still built for the old, smaller rooms. That left a gap in the outer wall, and a ledge outside it with nothing beyond. Closed.
- **STILL LIFE**: the arcade got wider but its entrance didn't, leaving a gap on each side between the doors and the first shops. Walled up.
- **OPEN SPACE**: just past the car park, the fields had a strip with no ground. There's ground now.
- **BLACKOUT**: a thin strip between Mill Road's pavement and the fence had no ground. Paved.
- The bus in BLACKOUT is solid.
- New test: `fall_probe` walks every reachable spot in every realm and reports anywhere you could step off into a drop.

## Update 6: "Unleaded"
BLACKOUT doesn't start in the lane any more. It starts under a petrol station's canopy, the only light for a mile, running on its own generator.
- The shop is lit and open. The till drawer is open, a tea is still warm, a test card is on the TV and a radio plays quietly.
- A car sits at pump 2 with its door open, engine running and headlights on, and nobody in it.
- Mill Road is dark, and you walk it streetlight to streetlight. You pass SUDS, a launderette open 24 hours with one drier turning a towel you know, and a bus shelter whose timetable lists every bus at 3:12. Then comes the turning into Larkspur Lane.
- At the end of the street, the dark no longer comes for you. The bus waits, lit up, with its doors open.

## Update 5: "Do Not Disturb"
In ABOVE, the freight lift opens onto the sixty-first floor of a hotel at night, not onto a bare roof.
- The corridor is red-carpeted. A television is on very quietly behind one door, and the ice machine hums.
- A room-service tray holds a slice of birthday cake with one candle, never lit.
- Room 6114 stands open, with the bed turned down and nothing outside the window but cloud.
- Stairs lead up to the roof terrace. It has water tanks, droning air-conditioning, a bar under strings of bulbs with two drinks whose ice hasn't melted, a helipad, and a warm pool above the clouds.
- A footbridge with railings crosses to the first rooftop.

## Update 4: "No Running By The Pool"
THE DEEP is now a whole leisure centre.
- In the lobby at night, the sea presses against the street doors.
- The changing rooms have a shower left running and a foot bath.
- Then come the pool rooms: white tile and shallow water, with daylight in the middle of the night.
  - a shallows hall under skylights;
  - a corridor with a channel down it and portholes onto the sky;
  - a hall whose far wall isn't there, where the water goes on to the horizon.
- A corridor where the daylight gives out leads into the pool hall at night.

## Update 3: "Cardio Day Cancelled"
No more chases, except one at the very end, in THE QUIET.
- Mannequins never leave their plinths. They change their stance while you aren't looking, and turn to face you.
- In the Backrooms, the thing behind the glass stays behind it, and a trail of red lamps leads you back to the lift.
- The leviathan passes over you and goes on.
- The tenement folds up behind you, but keeps its distance.
- The sheet figures shift where they stand, and never come closer.

## Update 2: "Out of Office"
The Backrooms got a long way in.
- You wake on the carpet of an office at night, with the blinds lit like day.
- A corridor of numbered doors follows; one is open, with a radio playing a song you know.
- A hall of pillars stretches a hundred metres across. Along the way: a staircase into the ceiling, a wall with windows onto a summer sky, and a payphone ringing.
- One last corridor leads into the yellow rooms. Once you're through, the doorway is wallpaper.
- The carpets and ceiling tiles are clean now, and the damp is laid on stain by stain, so it never repeats.

## Update 1: "Escape Room Escape"
Each realm now has one general aim ("find a way out") and no checklists.
- No more hunting for keys, handles, fuses, breaker orders or ring combinations: a door is a door.
- Everything is wider and more open, except COMPRESSION and the tenement, which are meant to be tight.
